Nathan Rininger is a Freshman studying Materials Science and Engineering
I feel like I've met a lot of wonderful people and I've made some good friends. It's hard because, again, coming to college, you're around completely new people. That you've never talked to before and you don't know anything about them. So it can definitely be intimidating, but when you can feel inclined to only talk to your friends at home because you already know them. I think it's about finding friends at college is really about coming out of your comfort zone and being vulnerable and taught having real conversations with other people. And I think I've been able to do that. I’ve been able to talk about some like pretty like serious topics with people and I've got to learn a lot about them. So, yeah, I think I found my people at college. I also feel independent a lot of the time I think I feel every day whenever I'm doing something. On my own, that could mean eating breakfast or getting food by myself or walking to class or sitting in a class that I don't know anyone in. Or working out at the Rec, I'm on my own there and those are times when I really feel independent and feel like an adult because I'm not really with anyone else in those moments. I'm just with myself and with my own thoughts. And a lot of the time, especially when I'm in class, I'm not on my cell phone, so I'm not texting anyone. Sometimes I feel like it's just me and the professor that I'm learning from. So that really feels like an independent experience.
