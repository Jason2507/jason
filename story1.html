Jonah Gould is a Freshman studying Mechanical Engineering and is part of the Formula SAE club
"I absolutely don't think it's a waste of time to go through with all these requirements because I took a class last semester and it was about how the environment affects human societies. And it's super eye-opening, a class I never would have taken because I would have thought it was just a waste of space in my schedule. You know, I could have taken some major courses, but I had to fill out my environmental designation, and it was really interesting to learn about how different communities are affected differently by climate change, and it just makes you more of a well-rounded and educated person who can understand the world more and better. I do think it's very important to get some real-world experience but do think it's still important to take your gen eds because I get my real-world experience from my clubs and extracurriculars. I got plenty of experience with hands-on work and designing. So, I really don't think that these extra classes are burdened."
